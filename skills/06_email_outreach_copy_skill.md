# Skill: Email Outreach Copy

**Used by:** Message Strategy Agent, Email Copy Agent  
**Domain:** B2B email copywriting for M1–M4 sequences

---

## Purpose
Provides angle library, persona-to-angle mapping, stage-specific format guidance, and subject line rules for B2B email outreach across M1–M4.

---

## When to use
When Email Copy Agent generates any email draft.

---

## Persona-to-angle library

| Persona | Core angle | Proof point type |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| CXO / Strategy | Market positioning, competitive risk, strategic opportunity | Market sizing data, sector benchmarks |
| Marketing | Brand intelligence, competitor messaging, customer insight | Campaign data, brand perception data |
| Operations | Efficiency benchmarks, cost data, process optimisation | Operational benchmarks, cost reduction case |
| Product / R&D | Innovation landscape, technology adoption, feature gap | Technology adoption data, patent landscape |
| Investor | Market sizing, growth projection, sector entry signal | TAM/SAM data, growth rate benchmarks |
| Procurement | Supplier landscape, pricing benchmarks, sourcing intelligence | Vendor comparison, cost benchmarks |

## Stage format guidance

### M1 — Cold first touch
- Length: 120–180 words
- Structure: Hook → Context → Value prop → Single CTA
- Subject: Question or insight-led (not "Following up")
- CTA: One clear ask (15-minute call, reply with interest, download)

### M2 — First follow-up
- Length: 100–150 words
- Structure: Reference M1 → One new proof point → Restate CTA
- Subject: Different angle from M1
- CTA: Same as M1 or lower-friction alternative

### M3 — Second follow-up
- Length: 100–130 words
- Structure: Social proof or sector trend → Soft urgency → CTA
- Subject: Insight or news hook
- CTA: Same ask or "just reply yes/no"

### M4 — Final nudge
- Length: 80–100 words
- Structure: Acknowledge persistence → Low-pressure offer → Exit gracefully
- Subject: "Last one from me" or resource offer
- CTA: Alternative value (article, report, no-strings ask)

---

## Subject line rules
- Under 60 characters
- No spam trigger words
- Each M1–M4 subject must be meaningfully different
- Avoid "Following up on my previous email" — too passive

---

## Rules
- No HTML in body (plain text only)
- No false claims or unverifiable statistics
- Signature must include sender name, title, company, and website

---

## Failure cases
- Copy that exceeds word limit → trim before compliance review
- Subject over 60 chars → shorten before submission
