# Word Choice Rules — Copy Agents Learning

Learned through Email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp agent development.

## ✓ MUST USE

**M1 Openers:**
- "quick insight"
- "just finished"
- "we completed"
- "we're seeing"
- "recently finished"

**Pain Points (Specific, Measurable):**
- "Dealer NPS declining as farm consolidation shifts power to bundled-solution agrochemical majors"
- "Teacher turnover at 20-year high, creating curriculum continuity gaps"
- "CAC inflation 30-40% YoY while iOS privacy changes blind attribution"

**Value Exchange:**
- "we walk through X, you tell us if it maps to Y"
- "we explore [finding], you tell us whether it applies to [their situation]"

**Follow-ups:**
- "No pressure"
- "Companies ahead are already..."
- "Interested?"
- "Still interested?"

**CTA Timeframes:**
- "30-min"
- "1-hour"
- "15 minutes"
- "quick 30-min call"

**Competitive Language:**
- "walked competitors through"
- "companies ahead"
- "leaders tracking this"
- "top performers"

---

## ✗ NEVER USE

**Weak Openers:**
- "compare notes" (vague, overused)
- "quick chat" (unprofessional, undefined)
- "happy to connect" (generic filler)
- "let me know" (weak follow-up, except WhatsApp soft asks)
- "worth a call" (no timeframe)
- "keen to discuss" (too eager)

**Generic Angles:**
- "market opportunities"
- "synergies"
- "leverage"
- "operational efficiency"
- "market challenges"
- "strategic alignment"

**Weak Pain Points:**
- Generic business jargon without specifics
- Unmeasurable or vague challenges

---

## Why This Matters

**Specificity drives credibility:** Real pain points (with numbers, industry context) signal research. Generic language signals lack of preparation.

**Forbidden phrases are weak:** They've been used so many times they signal low effort. Alternatives (walked competitors through, companies ahead) are stronger and more specific.

**Timeframe removes friction:** "30-min call" is concrete. "Call" is vague and creates hesitation.

---

## Pattern Recognition

**Weak:** "We'd like to compare notes on your approach."
**Strong:** "We walked competitors through our findings. They repositioned on dealer relationships. Worth a 30-minute call to explore if it maps to your strategy?"

**Weak:** "Happy to chat about market opportunities."
**Strong:** "We're seeing dealer NPS decline as farm consolidation shifts power. Worth 30 minutes to align on how you're positioning?"
