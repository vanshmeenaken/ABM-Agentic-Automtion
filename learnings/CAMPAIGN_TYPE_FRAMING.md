# Campaign Type Framing — Strategic Intent

Learned through Email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp agents. Each campaign type requires different strategic framing language.

---

## Campaign Types & Intent

### Survey

**Intent:** Gather intelligence on market/peer practices.

**Strategic Framing:**
- Email: "We recently finished a mandate on [Industry]"
- LinkedIn: "We are conducting research on [Industry]"
- WhatsApp: "quick insight from our research on [Industry]"

**Pain Focus:** Strategic blindspot (what leaders aren't tracking)

**Escalation (M3):** Second pain shows competitive gap

**Example:**
- M1 Pain: "Most leaders at your level aren't tracking dealer consolidation trend"
- M3 Escalation: "Leaders ahead are already repositioning on bundled solutions"

---

### POV (Point of View)

**Intent:** Share perspective/analysis, position thought leadership.

**Strategic Framing:**
- Email: "We recently completed intelligence research on [Industry]"
- LinkedIn: "We are developing a strategic POV on [Industry]"
- WhatsApp: "insights from our analysis on [Industry]"

**Pain Focus:** Strategic challenge requiring new approach

**Escalation (M3):** Market shift evidence

**Example:**
- M1 Pain: "Traditional dealer models can't compete with bundled solution majors"
- M3 Escalation: "Market has shifted — companies behind in repositioning already losing margin"

---

### Benchmarking

**Intent:** Compare performance across peer set.

**Strategic Framing:**
- Email: "We recently finished an intelligence report on [Industry]"
- LinkedIn: "We are benchmarking across [Industry]"
- WhatsApp: "benchmarking across [Industry]"

**Pain Focus:** Performance gap (how you compare to peers)

**Escalation (M3):** Urgency signal (accelerating, already moved)

**Example:**
- M1 Pain: "Cold chain operators cutting costs 25% faster than revenue growth"
- M3 Escalation: "Leaders moved on automation 18 months ago — window closing"

---

### Competition Benchmarking

**Intent:** Competitive intelligence gathering.

**Strategic Framing:**
- Email: "We recently completed competitive intelligence on [Industry]"
- LinkedIn: "We are benchmarking competitive positioning in [Industry]"
- WhatsApp: "competitive analysis in [Industry]"

**Pain Focus:** Competitive threat (what competitors are doing)

**Escalation (M3):** Market consolidation/reshuffling evidence

**Example:**
- M1 Pain: "Competitors walking in bundled solution with farmer engagement apps"
- M3 Escalation: "Market consolidation accelerating — leading players moving this quarter"

---

### Market Research

**Intent:** Trend analysis/market insights.

**Strategic Framing:**
- Email: "We recently finished market intelligence on [Industry]"
- LinkedIn: "We are analyzing market trends in [Industry]"
- WhatsApp: "market trends in [Industry]"

**Pain Focus:** Market trend (shift in buyer behavior, channel dynamics)

**Escalation (M3):** Urgency signal (accelerating, already moved, window closing)

**Example:**
- M1 Pain: "Buyer preferences shifting from price to integrated solutions"
- M3 Escalation: "Window closing — early movers gaining 30% market share advantage"

---

## How to Apply Framing

**Step 1:** Identify campaign type from input

**Step 2:** Use exact framing language from table above in M1 opener

**Step 3:** Ensure M1 pain aligns with campaign intent

**Step 4:** M3 escalation matches the threat/opportunity

**Example Workflow:**

Campaign Type: **Survey** on Crop Protection Pesticides

**Email M1 opener:**
> "We recently finished a mandate on Crop Protection Pesticides. While reviewing the findings, we discovered something most leaders at [Company]'s level aren't tracking yet..."

**LinkedIn M1 opener:**
> "We are conducting research on Crop Protection Pesticides. Our research addresses the gap between [PAIN_POINT_1] and [PAIN_POINT_2]."

**WhatsApp M1 opener:**
> "quick insight from our research on Crop Protection Pesticides: [PAIN_POINT_1]"
