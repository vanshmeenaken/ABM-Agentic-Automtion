# Channel Characteristics — Email vs LinkedIn vs WhatsApp

Learned through building three agents. Each channel requires different message structure, cadence, length, and tone.

---

## Email Copy Agent

**When to use:** Professional, formal, documented communication.

**Cadence:** Slower
- M1: Day 1
- M2: Day 3-4
- M3: Day 7-10

**Message Length:**
- M1: 100-150 words
- M2: 100-150 words
- M3: 100-150 words

**Structure:**
- Subject line (M1 unique, M2-M3 use "Re:" threading)
- Preview text (under 100 chars)
- Paragraph breaks (mobile readability)
- Signature

**Voice:**
- Professional
- Research-backed
- Formal but warm
- Thread-like (builds on M1)

**Unique Elements:**
- Subject lines maintain conversation
- M2-M3 use "Re:" to keep single thread
- Paragraph breaks improve mobile readability
- "Walked competitors through" language
- Specific research framing by campaign type

**CTA Pattern:**
- Confident, specific timeframe
- Value exchange clearly stated
- Example: "Would you be open to a 30-minute call? We walk you through the full picture, and you tell us if it maps to what you're seeing."

---

## LinkedIn Copy Agent

**When to use:** Peer-to-peer, relationship-based, social connection.

**Cadence:** Moderate
- M1: Day 1 (post-connection DM)
- M2: Day 3-4
- M3: Day 7-10

**Message Length:**
- M1: 80-120 words
- M2: 60-80 words
- M3: 80-100 words

**Structure:**
- DM only (no connection request note — send blank connection)
- Short lines (no dashes)
- One idea per paragraph
- Conversational opening ("Hi [name]")

**Voice:**
- Peer-to-peer
- Casual DM tone
- Conversational ("we're seeing", "we found")
- Direct but friendly
- Reference their planning cycle/season

**Unique Elements:**
- Post-connection DMs only
- Blank connection (no note)
- Short, punchy lines
- "Either way" pattern for M3
- One observation per paragraph

**CTA Pattern:**
- "Worth [timeframe] to [action]?"
- Example: "Would you be open to a 30-minute call? We walk you through what we found on how top performers approach this differently, and you tell us whether it maps to your [pain_point] at [Company]."

---

## WhatsApp Copy Agent

**When to use:** Urgent, mobile-first, high-frequency, intimate channel.

**Cadence:** Fast
- M1: Day 1
- M2: Day 2-3
- M3: Day 5-7

**Message Length:**
- M1: 25-40 words per bubble
- M2: 25-40 words per bubble
- M3: 25-40 words per bubble
- Multiple bubbles = multiple ideas

**Structure:**
- Multiple message bubbles (one idea per bubble)
- No subject lines
- No dashes (use line breaks)
- Follow-ups: M1 has 2, M2 has 1, M3 has none

**Voice:**
- Conversational, direct, urgent
- Very casual
- Mobile-native
- Quick insights
- No formality

**Unique Elements:**
- One message bubble = one idea (mobile natural breaks)
- Follow-ups built into cadence
- M1 Follow-ups: 2 ("No pressure...", "Still interested?")
- M2 Follow-up: 1 ("Let me know once...")
- No ALL CAPS or excessive punctuation
- No emojis (professional)
- Line breaks instead of dashes

**CTA Pattern:**
- "Would you be open to a quick 30-min call? We walk through what we found."
- Quick, direct
- Example M3: "Either way, let's connect once. [Pain point] is shifting fast — worth aligning on how you're thinking about it. Open to 30 min?"

## Why Channel Matters

**Email** = formal, documented. Slower pace. Research signals matter. Threading maintains context.

**LinkedIn** = social, relationship. Peer voice. DM intimacy. Can reference external context (their timeline, their posts).

**WhatsApp** = immediate, intimate, mobile. Urgency built in. Short attention span. Multiple touches required.

**Cadence differences:** Email slower because it's formal channel. WhatsApp faster because it's mobile + intimate + high-frequency acceptable.

**Word count differences:** Email can be dense (100-150 words). LinkedIn shorter on M2 (60-80) because DM platform. WhatsApp shortest (25-40) because mobile scanning.

**Follow-up differences:** Email M1-M3 all standalone. LinkedIn M1-M3 all standalone. WhatsApp uses follow-ups as separate messages (M1: 2 follow-ups, M2: 1 follow-up) because multiple touches are natural on WhatsApp.
