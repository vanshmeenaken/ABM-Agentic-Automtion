# CTA Rules — Strict Requirements

Learned through testing Email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp agents. These rules are NON-NEGOTIABLE.

---

## Rule 1: ALWAYS Name Timeframe

**What:** Every CTA must specify exact duration.

**NOT:** "Would you be open to a call?"
**YES:** "Would you be open to a 30-minute call?"

**Valid timeframes:**
- 30-min call
- 1-hour
- 15 minutes
- quick 30-min

**Why:** Removes ambiguity. Prospect knows exactly what they're committing to.

---

## Rule 2: ALWAYS Include Value Exchange

**What:** Must explain what they get, what you get.

**NOT:** "Let me tell you what we found."
**YES:** "We walk you through what we found on how top performers approach this, and you tell us whether it maps to your situation at [Company]."

**Pattern:** "We [do X], you tell us if it [maps to / applies to / aligns with] your [Y]"

**Why:** Removes pressure. Makes it mutual conversation, not a pitch.

---

## Rule 3: ALWAYS Reference THEIR Context

**What:** Anchor CTA to their specific situation, season, planning cycle.

**NOT:** "Would you be open to a call?"
**YES:** "Would you be open to a 30-minute call? We explore how [their specific challenge], and you tell us if it maps to what you're seeing in [their region/industry/planning cycle]."

**Context types:**
- Planning cycle ("in your H1 planning")
- Season ("as you head into budgeting season")
- Specific pain ("as farm consolidation shifts power")
- Competitive shift ("as iOS privacy changes reshape attribution")
- Their company ("at Syngenta's scale")

**Why:** Shows you did homework. Generic CTAs feel cold.

---

## Rule 4: M3 MUST Use "Either Way" Opener

**What:** M3 closes with low-pressure, peer-to-peer pattern.

**Pattern:**
> "Either way, let's connect once. [Secondary pain point observation]. Worth 30 minutes to align on [specific ask]?"

**Examples:**

Email:
> "Either way, let's connect. Margin compression is accelerating — companies ahead? Already repositioning. Worth 30 minutes to compare notes on how you're thinking about it."

LinkedIn:
> "Either way, let's talk once. [Secondary pain] is shifting fast — worth aligning on how you're thinking about it."

WhatsApp:
> "Either way, let's connect once. [Pain point] is shifting fast — worth aligning on how you're thinking about it."

**Why:** Low-pressure. Peer observation (not pushy). Acknowledges they might be busy but signals opportunity cost.

---

## Rule 5: CTA Must Have Value Exchange + Timeframe + Context

**All three together = strong CTA**

**Weak (missing timeframe):**
> "Would you be open to connecting?"

**Weak (missing value exchange):**
> "Would you be open to a 30-minute call to discuss dealer consolidation?"

**Weak (missing context):**
> "We walk you through our research, and you tell us if it maps to your strategy. Worth a 30-minute call?"

**Strong (all three):**
> "Would you be open to a 30-minute call? We walk through how top performers at your scale are repositioning on dealer relationships, and you tell us if it maps to what you're seeing in your regions."

---

## Quality Checklist (CTA Section)

- [ ] M1 CTA names specific timeframe (30-min, 1-hour, etc.)
- [ ] M1 CTA includes value exchange ("we X, you tell us Y")
- [ ] M1 CTA references their context (planning cycle, season, pain, metric)
- [ ] M2 has soft ask (no pressure language)
- [ ] M3 opens with "Either way"
- [ ] M3 escalates with secondary pain point
- [ ] M3 has specific CTA with timeframe
- [ ] No forbidden language in any CTA (compare notes, quick chat, let me know, etc.)
