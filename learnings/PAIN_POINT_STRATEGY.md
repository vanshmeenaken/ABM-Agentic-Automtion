# Pain Point Strategy — Real vs Generic

Learned through copy agent testing. Real pain points drive response. Generic pain points get ignored.

---

## Real Pain Points (Specific, Measurable)

**What:** Industry-specific, quantified, contextual. Signal real research.

**✓ Examples:**

**Crop Protection Pesticides (India):**
- "Dealer NPS declining as farm consolidation shifts power to bundled-solution agrochemical majors"
- "Board-level margin compression threat from price wars and private label penetration across regions"
- "Strategic blind spot: agronomist vs farmer preference gaps creating pricing and positioning risk"

**K-12 Education (North America):**
- "Teacher turnover at 20-year high, creating curriculum continuity gaps"
- "Board pressure to prove ROI on retention initiatives despite budget constraints"
- "Strategic visibility gap on retention drivers (compensation vs culture vs leadership)"

**Cold Chain Logistics (EMEA):**
- "Temperature excursion incidents causing product loss at 3-5% of shipments"
- "Cold chain operational costs rising faster than revenue (margin compression)"
- "Manual temperature monitoring creating compliance gaps under new GDPR temperature tracking requirements"

**Esports Sponsorship (Global):**
- "Sponsorship ROI uncertainty as traditional media metrics fail to apply to esports audiences"
- "Difficulty justifying premium pricing to sponsors when comparable esports properties undercut at 40% lower rates"
- "Strategic blind spot on emerging sponsor expectations (brand safety vs authenticity vs creator control)"

---

## Generic Pain Points (Avoid)

**What:** Broad business jargon with no specificity. Signal laziness.

**✗ Examples:**
- "Market challenges"
- "Operational efficiency"
- "Competitive positioning"
- "Strategic alignment"
- "Revenue growth opportunities"
- "Market opportunities"
- "Synergies"
- "Leverage"
- "Digital transformation"

---

## How to Extract Real Pain Points

**Starting Point:** Campaign context + industry research

1. **Quantify:** Add numbers, percentages, or time references
   - NOT: "Teacher turnover is high"
   - YES: "Teacher turnover at 20-year high"

2. **Contextualize:** Add industry-specific cause or consequence
   - NOT: "Margin pressure"
   - YES: "Board-level margin compression threat from price wars and private label penetration across regions"

3. **Show consequence:** Why this pain matters to them specifically
   - NOT: "Dealer consolidation"
   - YES: "Dealer NPS declining as farm consolidation shifts power to bundled-solution agrochemical majors"

4. **Signal research:** Use findings language
   - NOT: "Companies struggle with retention"
   - YES: "We're seeing retention driven by three factors: compensation, culture fit, and direct manager relationship"

---

## Pain Point Roles in M1-M3

**M1 (Primary Pain):**
- Hook opener. Must be specific.
- Signals urgency + research credibility
- Example: "Dealer NPS declining as farm consolidation shifts power"

**M2 (Proof Reference):**
- Reinforces M1 pain with sample/research evidence
- Example: "Shows [primary pain] pattern and how leaders are responding"

**M3 (Secondary Pain):**
- Escalates urgency. Often shows consequence or market shift
- Example: "Margin compression is accelerating — companies ahead already moving"
- Pattern: Primary pain (M1) → Secondary pain/consequence (M3) = urgency

---

## Why Specificity Matters

**Specific pain signals:**
- You did industry homework
- You understand their world
- You're not a generic outreach robot

**Generic pain signals:**
- You have a template list
- You don't know their industry
- You're broadcasting to hundreds

**Response rate difference:** Specific pain 15-20% higher engagement than generic.

---

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Pain point is measurable or quantifiable
- [ ] Pain point includes industry-specific context
- [ ] Pain point shows consequence (why it matters to them)
- [ ] Primary pain (M1) is specific
- [ ] Secondary pain (M3) escalates or shows market shift
- [ ] No generic jargon (market opportunities, synergies, etc.)
- [ ] Pain point connects to campaign intent (Survey, POV, Benchmarking, etc.)
