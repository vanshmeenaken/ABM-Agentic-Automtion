## Word Choice & Language Rules

### MUST USE:
- **M1 openers:** "quick insight", "just finished", "we completed"
- **Pain points:** Specific, measurable (not "market challenges" — use actual pain like "Dealer NPS declining")
- **Value exchange:** "we walk through X, you tell us if it maps to Y"
- **Follow-ups:** "No pressure", "Companies ahead are already...", "Interested?"
- **CTA timeframes:** "30-min", "1-hour", "quick call" (always specify)

### NEVER USE:
- ✗ "Compare notes" (overused, weak CTA)
- ✗ "Quick chat" (vague, not professional)
- ✗ "Happy to connect" (generic filler)
- ✗ "Let me know" (weak follow-up)
- ✗ "Worth a call" (no timeframe)
- ✗ "Keen to discuss" (too eager)
- ✗ Generic angles like "market opportunities" (use specific pain points)

### TONE RULES:
- No ALL CAPS or excessive punctuation (!!!???)
- No emojis (keep professional)
- No dashes for pauses (use line breaks instead)
- One idea per message bubble
- Conversational but business-focused
