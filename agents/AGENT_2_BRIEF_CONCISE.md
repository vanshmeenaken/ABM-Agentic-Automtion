# Market-Aware Messaging Strategy Generation

## Deep Market Analysis
- Analyzes campaign context + target industry to extract REAL pain points specific to that market
- Not generic persona templates ("CXO cares about ROI")
- Contextual understanding of industry dynamics driving decision-making
- Claude CLI powered for intelligent market interpretation

## Pain Point Extraction Per Persona
- Identifies 3 specific pain points each persona faces in the target industry
- Pain points tied to campaign type and industry context
- Example: K-12 CXO pain = "competitive EdTech adoption disadvantage"; Cold Chain VP Ops pain = "margin erosion from utilization gaps"
- Pain points are actionable and tied to the campaign offer

## Persona-Specific Messaging Strategy
- Primary angle: Specific messaging hook that resonates with THIS persona in THIS market
- Value proposition: How the campaign offer solves their specific pain points (not generic benefits)
- Persona-specific approach: Different angles for CXO vs Director vs Manager in same campaign

## Campaign-Aware Tone & Positioning
- Determines messaging tone based on campaign type (consultative, professional, competitive, etc.)
- Tone matches campaign objectives and industry context
- Example: Market Research = consultative tone; Competition Benchmarking = competitive, urgent tone

## Channel-Specific Messaging Guidance
- Email guidance: Tone, structure, data-driven elements required
- WhatsApp guidance: Conversational, warm, peer-to-peer messaging style
- LinkedIn guidance: Thought leadership angles, professional tone, narrative approach

## Campaign Success Criteria
- Generates realistic success metrics based on campaign type and industry
- Example: Market Research targets 28-35% email open rate + 1-2% meeting rate
- Metrics inform Email Copy Agent performance expectations

## Campaign-Type Aware Strategy
- Supports multiple campaign types: Market Research, Survey, Competition Benchmarking, POV
- Different strategy patterns for each campaign type
- Routes to appropriate message series (skill-based, logic-based, or contextual patterns)

---

**Capability: Generates market-intelligent messaging strategies that guide all downstream campaign copy and channel delivery**
