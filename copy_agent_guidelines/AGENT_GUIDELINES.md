# Copy Agent Guidelines — Email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp

Master guidelines for all three copy agents (Email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp). Each agent generates M1-M3 series with frozen templates + strategic framing.

---

## Core Principles (All Agents)

### M1-M3 Series Structure
- **M1 (Day 1/Day 1):** Hook + specific CTA + follow-ups
- **M2 (Day 3-4/Day 2-3):** Proof/sample + soft ask + follow-ups
- **M3 (Day 7-10/Day 5-7):** "Either way" close + escalation + specific CTA

### CTA Rules (STRICT - All Agents)
1. **ALWAYS name timeframe:** "30-min call", "1-hour", "15 minutes" — NEVER just "call" or "chat"
2. **ALWAYS include value exchange:** "we walk through X, you tell us if it maps to Y"
3. **ALWAYS reference THEIR context:** Planning cycle, season, specific pain, metric
4. **M3 MUST use "Either way":** Low-pressure opener
5. **NEVER use:** "compare notes", "quick chat", "happy to connect", "let me know", "worth a call"

### Personalization (All Agents)
- Prospect first name: "Hi Rajesh"
- Company name: "mapped to Syngenta India"
- Industry context: "research on Crop Protection Pesticides"
- Region context: "across India"

## Email Copy Agent

### Channel Characteristics
- **Cadence:** Day 1 → Day 3-4 → Day 7-10 (slower, professional)
- **Length:** 100-150 words per email
- **Threading:** M2-M3 use "Re:" to maintain single conversation thread
- **Structure:** Subject line + preview text + body + CTA

### M1 Hook Variants
**Variant 1:** "Most leaders missing: [pain_point]" (FOMO)  
**Variant 2:** "Window closing on [pain_point]" (scarcity)

### Email-Specific Rules
- ✓ Subject lines are unique (M1) or "Re:" threaded (M2-M3)
- ✓ Preview text under 100 chars
- ✓ Paragraph breaks (readability on mobile)
- ✓ "Walked competitors through" (not "peer")
- ✓ Specific opening: "We recently finished a mandate on [Industry]"

### M1 Body Pattern (Variant 1)
```
Hi [first_name],

We recently finished a mandate on [Industry]. While reviewing the findings, we discovered something most leaders at [Company]'s level aren't tracking yet:

[PAIN_POINT_1]

The companies ahead? They're already moving on this. We walked competitors through what we found, and it shifted how they're positioning on [PAIN_POINT_2].

Would you be open to a 30-minute call? We walk you through the full picture, and you tell us if it maps to what you're seeing.

Looking forward to connecting.

Best
```

## LinkedIn Copy Agent

### Channel Characteristics
- **Cadence:** Day 1 → Day 3-4 → Day 7-10 (post-connection DMs only)
- **Length:** 80-120 words M1, 60-80 words M2, 80-100 words M3
- **Voice:** Peer-to-peer, casual DM tone
- **No connection request note:** Send blank connection, DM series only

### LinkedIn-Specific Rules
- ✓ Short, punchy lines (no dashes)
- ✓ Conversational tone ("Hi Rajesh, we're seeing...")
- ✓ "Either way" pattern for M3
- ✓ One idea per paragraph
- ✓ Reference their planning cycle/season

### M1 Body Pattern (Variant 1)
```
Hi [first_name], [PAIN_POINT_1].

We are conducting research on [Industry]. Our research addresses the gap between [PAIN_POINT_1] and [PAIN_POINT_2].

Would you be open to a 30-minute call? We walk you through what we found on how top performers approach this differently, and you tell us whether it maps to your [PAIN_POINT_2] at [Company].
```

## WhatsApp Copy Agent

### Channel Characteristics
- **Cadence:** Day 1 → Day 2-3 → Day 5-7 (faster, mobile-native)
- **Length:** 25-40 words per message (one idea per bubble)
- **Voice:** Very conversational, direct, urgent
- **Follow-ups:** M1 has 2 follow-ups, M2 has 1 follow-up

### WhatsApp-Specific Rules
- ✓ No ALL CAPS or excessive punctuation
- ✓ No emojis (keep professional)
- ✓ Line breaks instead of dashes
- ✓ One message bubble = one idea
- ✓ Quick CTAs ("30-min call", "1-hour")

### M1 Body Pattern (Variant 1)
```
Hi [first_name], quick insight from our research on [Industry]:

[PAIN_POINT_1]

Would you be open to a quick 30-min call to explore? We walk through what we found.
```

### Follow-up Examples
- M1 Follow-up 1: "No pressure — just thought this mapped to [Company]."
- M1 Follow-up 2: "Still interested in a quick chat?"
- M2 Follow-up: "Let me know once you've had a chance to look."

## Word Choice Rules (All Agents)

### ✓ MUST USE
- **M1 openers:** "quick insight", "just finished", "we completed", "we're seeing"
- **Pain points:** Specific, measurable (NOT "market challenges", "operational efficiency")
- **Value exchange:** "we walk through X, you tell us if it maps to Y"
- **Follow-ups:** "No pressure", "Companies ahead are already...", "Interested?", "Still interested?"
- **CTA timeframes:** "30-min", "1-hour", "15 minutes", "quick 30-min call"
- **Competitive language:** "walked competitors through", "companies ahead", "leaders tracking this"

### ✗ NEVER USE
- "Compare notes" — weak, overused
- "Quick chat" — vague, unprofessional
- "Happy to connect" — generic filler
- "Let me know" — weak follow-up (except in WhatsApp soft asks)
- "Worth a call" — no timeframe
- "Keen to discuss" — too eager
- "Generic angles" — avoid "market opportunities", "synergies", "leverage"

### TONE RULES (All Agents)
- No ALL CAPS except emphasis (WhatsApp only)
- No excessive punctuation (!!!???)
- No dashes for pauses (use line breaks)
- Conversational but business-focused
- Direct, specific language
- Avoid hedging ("might", "could", "perhaps")

## Campaign Type Intent

### Survey
- **Intent:** Gather intelligence on market/peer practices
- **Framing:** "We recently finished a mandate on [Industry]"
- **Pain focus:** Strategic blindspot (what leaders aren't tracking)
- **Escalation:** Second pain shows competitive gap

### POV
- **Intent:** Share perspective/analysis
- **Framing:** "We recently completed intelligence research on [Industry]"
- **Pain focus:** Strategic challenge requiring new approach
- **Escalation:** Market shift evidence

### Competition Benchmarking
- **Intent:** Competitive intelligence gathering
- **Framing:** "We recently completed competitive intelligence on [Industry]"
- **Pain focus:** Competitive threat (what competitors are doing)
- **Escalation:** Market consolidation/reshuffling evidence

### Market Research
- **Intent:** Trend analysis/market insights
- **Framing:** "We recently finished market intelligence on [Industry]"
- **Pain focus:** Market trend (shift in buyer behavior, channel dynamics)
- **Escalation:** Urgency signal (accelerating, already moved, window closing)

## Quality Checklist

All agents must pass EVERY check before approval:

- [ ] M1 opens with specific hook (pain point OR competitive threat)
- [ ] M1 has specific timeframe (30-min, 1-hour, etc.)
- [ ] M1 has value exchange ("we X, you tell us if Y")
- [ ] M2 includes proof reference (sample, attachment, research)
- [ ] M2 has soft ask (no pressure)
- [ ] M3 uses "Either way" opener
- [ ] M3 escalates with secondary pain point + urgency
- [ ] All CTAs name specific timeframe
- [ ] No forbidden language (compare notes, quick chat, let me know, etc.)
- [ ] Follow-ups included (M1 + M2)
- [ ] Conversational tone (appropriate to channel)
- [ ] Personalizations present (name, company, industry)
- [ ] Word counts in range (Email: 100-150, LinkedIn: 80-120/60-80/80-100, WhatsApp: 25-40)
- [ ] Campaign type-specific framing applied
- [ ] Pain points specific, not generic
